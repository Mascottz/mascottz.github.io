CEO & Founder

MasTECH Innovations

A Rare Blend of Software Engineer & Hardware Specialist

The founder of MasTECH Innovations brings a unique dual competency that defines the company's edge: deep expertise in full-stack software development — spanning React, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, and cloud architecture — combined with hands-on mastery of hardware systems, multi-device configurations, and enterprise IT infrastructure.

This rare combination means MasTECH doesn't just build your software — we understand the machines it runs on, the networks that connect them, and the physical infrastructure that keeps your operations alive. From architecting database schemas to replacing a motherboard flex cable, the CEO leads by example across every discipline the company serves.

Based in Dutse-Alhaji, FCT Abuja, the founder is driven by a mission to bridge the gap between world-class software engineering and reliable, accessible hardware support for businesses across Nigeria — eliminating the need to juggle multiple vendors for interconnected technology needs.
